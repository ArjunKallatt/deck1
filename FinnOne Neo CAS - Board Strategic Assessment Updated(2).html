Section 1 · Executive Context

Why loan origination has become a strategic growth platform

“Three forces have converged - and they all favour the Board treating CAS as a growth platform, not a productivity tool.”

1

Customer acquisition has moved to the phone

Offers, approvals and disbursals are now expected in-context and in minutes, not days. As a result, the LOS is what defines customer LTV, not back-office throughput, the way it used to.

Nucleus 2023 · IBS Intelligence 2024
2

Compliance-native origination is now the fastest path into new partnerships

RBI, SAMA and CBUAE now push auditability and vendor governance up to the origination layer itself. Banks that build this in natively move faster into open-finance ecosystems and new distribution partnerships - those that don't get locked out or slowed down.

RBI · EIOPA · SAMA · CBUAE
3

Falling infrastructure cost is funding new acquisition channels

As infrastructure and maintenance costs compress, savings are being redirected into faster onboarding and new acquisition channels, not back-office overhead. Celent notes LOS technology has changed more in five years than the prior twenty, with the market heading toward $7Bn by 2030.

Celent 2024 · Spherical Insights · VMI
Frame for the Board.
“The LOS is no longer a judgment engine. It is a channel - and increasingly the most important one banks have.”

The category is being redefined · LOS → Customer Acquisition Platform

Functional scope is widening - from origination decisions to lead management, omnichannel capture, embedded finance and AI-driven risk. Winning vendors are measured on customer-acquisition metrics (conversion, time-to-yes) - not just workflow throughput.

Celent 2024 · IBS Intelligence 2024 · ABI 2024
A

AI & decision intelligence

Underwriting moving rules-engine → model-driven. Explainability, MLOps and bureau orchestration now table-stakes for new RFPs.

FSI 2024 · Gartner 2024 · IBS Intelligence
B

Cloud-native SaaS economics

Multi-tenant SaaS origination (nCino, Mambu, Blend) compresses deployment 12–24 mo → 8–12 weeks. On-prem economics erode - consumption-based monetisation rises.

ABI Research · Celent 2024 · Aloan 2026
C

Open Finance & embedded lending

CBDC frameworks (RBI ULI, MAS Project Orchid), Pay-Later regulations and Open Finance (UAE/SAMA) enable third-party origination inside non-bank surfaces - the LOS becomes an API.

CBUAE 2024 · SAMA · RBI Digital Lending 2025
