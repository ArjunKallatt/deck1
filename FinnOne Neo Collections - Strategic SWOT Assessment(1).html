Section 3 · Executive SWOT

Executive SWOT - earned, not assumed

FinnOne Neo® Collections is a strong, reliable platform - good at automation, workflow and staying compliant. Where it falls behind is AI: today it mostly follows rules rather than making smart predictions, and it doesn't give borrowers much of a self-service option. The collections-software market is growing fast - about $4.8 Bn in 2025, heading toward $11.3 Bn by 2033 - and rules on AI in debt collection keep tightening.

Board Takeaway -
the verdict in one sentence

Our strengths in automation and compliance match what banks need most - efficiency and governance - but we need to move faster on AI and customer experience to keep intelligent new entrants from taking the deals we'd normally win.
